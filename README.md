## Key Performance Indicators & Visual Breakdown

1. **High-Level Business KPIs (Cards):**
   * **Sum of Quantity:** Total volume sold (1.783K units).
   * **Sum of Profit:** Total net financial returns (18K).
   * **Sum of Amount:** Total transaction value or gross revenue (139K).

2. **Geographical Insights:**
   * **Sum of Profit by State:** Horizontal bar chart highlighting top-performing regions (led by Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh).

3. **Temporal Trends:**
   * **Sum of Profit by Month:** Performance trajectory tracking financial returns across early-quarter months (January, February, and March).

4. **Customer & Category Analysis:**
   * **Sum of Amount by CustomerName:** Identifies high-value customers driving top sales volume.
   * **Sum of Quantity by Category:** Donut chart breaking down sales distribution across *Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture*.

5. **Operational Metrics & Preferences:**
   * **Sum of Quantity by PaymentMode:** Evaluates customer preferences across payment channels (COD, UPI, EMI, Debit Card, and Credit Card).
   * **Sum of Profit by Sub-Category:** Detailed profitability ranking across product sub-categories like *Printers, Phones, Bookcases, Accessories, and Stole*.

6. **Interactive Filters:**
   * **Quarter Slicers (`Qtr 1` to `Qtr 4`)** and **State Filter Dropdown** for dynamic reporting and granular analysis.
